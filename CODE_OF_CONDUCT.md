# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and take review comments as feedback on the work rather than the person, and keep disagreements about methods and evidence constructive. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, review comments, or any other project space.

Behavioral science is a field with real disagreements about frameworks and evidence. Those disagreements are welcome here when they are specific, cited, and about the work.

To report a concern, contact any maintainer listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Reports are handled by the maintainers of the organization not involved in the concern where possible, and kept confidential.
