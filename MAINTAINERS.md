## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are added by agreement of the existing maintainers from both organizations, typically after someone has authored or reviewed several skills. Open an issue or raise it at the monthly meeting.

## Responsibilities

- Review pull requests for content and structure (see `GOVERNANCE.md`).
- Attend the monthly meeting and take review assignments.
- Take part in the quarterly review of published skills.
- Keep `taxonomy.yaml`, the meta-skills, and the tooling current.
- Respond to issues within a reasonable time.
