# Evidence notes: draft-belonging-story

Every source below comes from `docs/plan/2026-10-skill-expansion-dossier.md` (compiled 2026-10-12). Verification tags are carried over as the dossier gives them; nothing has been upgraded or added from memory. Numbers appear only as the dossier writes them.

## Framework

### Walton & Wilson (2018). Wise interventions: Psychological remedies for social and personal problems [verified-crossref]
*Psychological Review*, 125(5), 617–655. https://doi.org/10.1037/rev0000115.
- The framework: brief, precise interventions that change a specific meaning people make of themselves or their situation. A belonging story targets one meaning: that an early setback shows people like the recipient do not belong. The saying-is-believing step and the single delivery follow this logic.
- The dossier records no numbers from this paper.

## Anchor trials (US college)

### Walton & Cohen (2011). A brief social-belonging intervention improves academic and health outcomes of minority students [verified-crossref]
*Science*, 331(6023), 1447–1451. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1198364.
- Original study, N = 92, minority students at a US college. The achievement gap roughly halved over 3 years.
- This is the largest effect in the record and comes from the smallest sample; the skill quotes it next to the 2023 result, never alone.

### Walton et al. (2023). Where and with whom does a brief social-belonging intervention promote progress in college? [verified-crossref]
*Science*, 380(6644), 499–505. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ade4420.
- 26,911 students at 22 institutions. Raised full-time first-year completion only where the group was afforded opportunities to belong. This finding is the affordance gate in the skill.
- Crossref lists an erratum (10.1126/science.ads9718, 2024); the author should check what it changes before quoting the numbers in a published skill (dossier correction 19).

## Protocol elements (from the dossier's boundary conditions)
- Collect real stories from older peers of the same group.
- Frame the difficulty as normal and as improving over time.
- Include the saying-is-believing step: the recipient writes advice to future peers.
- Deliver before the transition.
- Check that the context affords belonging.
- The dossier gives these as boundary conditions without separate citations; the four-part story criterion (difficulty, doubt, change, sign it was common) and the "collected, never written" rule are this skill's operationalization of them.

## Cross-cutting cautions (dossier section A)

### DellaVigna & Linos (2022). RCTs to scale: Comprehensive evidence from two nudge units [verified-crossref]
*Econometrica*, 90(1), 81–116. https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA18709.
- 126 RCTs, 23 million people. Academic journal studies average 8.7 percentage points (+33.4%); nudge-unit trials average 1.4 points (+8.0%). About 70% of the gap is explained by selective publication combined with low power.

### Ferraro & Tracy (2022). Loss-framed incentive contracts: meta-analysis and real-effort experiment [verified-crossref]
*Experimental Economics*, 25(5), 1441–1466. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10683-022-09754-x.
- Pooled 0.16 SD, but 0.33 SD in the lab versus 0.02 SD in the field, with publication bias among lab studies. The dossier's lab-to-field shrinkage example; the skill uses it with DellaVigna & Linos for the "discount published effects" point.

## Non-WEIRD evidence
- None found in the sources this skill draws on. The dossier states: "none found in the three reports". No trial of a belonging story in a low- or middle-income country is recorded, so the first use there is a test, not a rollout.
- The dossier's caution for the drafter: all evidence is US college; say so. The skill says so in the description, the WEIRD skew line, and the mandatory evidence statement.

## Replication status
- Replicated, with conditions (context affordance). N = 92 original; 26,911 students at 22 institutions replicated the effect only where the group was afforded opportunities to belong.

## Known corrections and cautions (from the dossier)
- Walton et al. (2023) has an erratum; author to check what it changes.
- The dossier lists no practitioner source for this skill.
- The dossier gives no effect size for the 2023 study beyond "raised full-time first-year completion" under the affordance condition; the skill quotes no percentage for it.

## What to check locally
- Which group line matters here (region, ethnicity, caste, language, gender, first generation); the stories must come from older peers on the same side of that line.
- Whether the setting affords belonging in fact: named people who will include the group, and no reported hostility. This is the check most often skipped, and the one the 2023 study makes decisive.
- Whether "belonging" has a local idiom, or whether people phrase the doubt as something else ("this is not for people from the villages").
- Whether written stories work at the reading level, or whether a session led by older peers, or audio, is needed.
- Whether older peers of the group exist in the setting at all; if the group is the first of its kind there, the technique cannot run as tested.
- The timing of the first setback in this program's calendar, so delivery can precede it.
- Whether persistence or completion can be measured at a named point, with a comparison group, and pre-registered.
