## Cross-cutting cautions (dossier section A)
- Not quoted in the skill: it describes an intervention and makes no effect claim. One connection is worth stating for users: DellaVigna & Linos (2022) [verified-crossref] attribute about 70% of the gap between academic-journal and nudge-unit effects (8.7 against 1.4 percentage points) to selective publication combined with low power; a description that separates how well an intervention was planned from how well it was delivered is part of what lets a reader judge what a reported effect was an effect of.

## What to check locally
1. Whether a written design document exists at all, or the description will be a labeled reconstruction.
2. Whether the materials in their final delivery languages are in one place a reader can reach.
3. Whether the context features a reader elsewhere would assume away (clinic hours, agent networks, who the messenger was, what the channel costs the participant) are written into item 7.
4. Whether the adaptation record was kept during delivery, or modifications will have to be reconstructed.
5. Whether fidelity data exist for each essential component, or item 12 will say "not measured".
6. Whether the description's destination (registry, journal) triggers the methodologist's check.
