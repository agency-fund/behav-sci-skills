# Evidence notes: select-messenger

Annotated bibliography for the skill. Every entry is copied from the library's evidence dossier (`docs/plan/2026-10-skill-expansion-dossier.md`, entry `select-messenger`, plus sections A and B) with the dossier's verification tag. Nothing was added from memory; a number appears only as the dossier writes it. "Unverified, author to confirm" means neither a Crossref nor a fetch check succeeded, and the item must not be cited as established.

## Anchor sources

**Dolan, P., Hallsworth, M., Halpern, D., King, D., Metcalfe, R., & Vlaev, I. (2012).** Influencing behaviour: The mindspace way. *Journal of Economic Psychology*, 33(1), 264–277. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joep.2011.10.009 [verified-crossref]
- The framework paper. MINDSPACE names nine levers (Messenger, Incentives, Norms, Defaults, Salience, Priming, Affect, Commitments, Ego). "Messenger" is the claim that people weight information by who delivers it. Written for UK policy; the dossier records no effect sizes from it and the skill quotes none.

**Banerjee, A., Chandrasekhar, A. G., Duflo, E., & Jackson, M. O. (2019).** Using gossips to spread information: Theory and evidence from two randomized controlled trials. *The Review of Economic Studies*. https://doi.org/10.1093/restud/rdz008 [verified-crossref] (plan-only citation; the audit confirms the citation, not its results)
- The source of the nomination approach: ask community members who is good at spreading information, and seed the message with the people they name. The dossier places the trials in Karnataka, India, and states that no results from them are in the reports. The skill uses the method and quotes no numbers.

**Banerjee, A., Chandrasekhar, A. G., Dalpath, S., Duflo, E., Floretta, J., Jackson, M. O., et al. (2025).** Selecting the most effective nudge: Evidence from a large-scale experiment on immunization. *Econometrica*, 93(4), 1183–1223. https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA19739 [verified-crossref]; NBER Working Paper 28726, https://doi.org/10.3386/w28726 [verified-crossref]
- Haryana, India. A large multi-arm trial on childhood immunization. The dossier's numbers for this skill: information-hub "ambassadors" nominated by the community plus SMS reminders were the most cost-effective option, at +9.1% immunizations per dollar; hubs improved full immunization; SMS alone had no impact. All three statements come from the J-PAL evaluation page, a secondary source per the report, which the audit did not re-fetch. The dossier's `select-messenger` entry cites this paper by journal, volume, and pages; the title, author list, and NBER version are taken from the same paper's entry under `design-reminder-schedule`.

## Cross-cutting cautions (dossier section A)

- **DellaVigna, S., & Linos, E. (2022).** RCTs to scale: Comprehensive evidence from two nudge units. *Econometrica*, 90(1), 81–116. https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA18709 [verified-crossref]. 126 RCTs, 23 million people. Academic journal studies average 8.7 percentage points (+33.4%); nudge-unit trials average 1.4 points (+8.0%). About 70% of the gap is selective publication plus low power.
- **Mertens, S., et al. (2022).** PNAS, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2107346118 [verified-crossref]: d = 0.45 (95% CI 0.39–0.52), more than 200 studies, N about 2.15 million, moderate publication bias. **Maier, M., et al. (2022).** PNAS, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2200300119 [verified-crossref]: bias-corrected average about d = 0.04. **Szaszi, B., et al. (2022).** PNAS letter, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2200732119 [verified-crossref]: bias-adjusted range d about 0.03–0.31; the average hides heterogeneity.
- **Bryan, C. J., Tipton, E., & Yeager, D. S. (2021).** Behavioural science is unlikely to change the world without a heterogeneity revolution. *Nature Human Behaviour*, 5(8), 980–989. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01143-3 [verified-crossref]. Expect heterogeneous effects; measure moderators. The dossier notes the fetched text does not explicitly discuss WEIRD samples, so no WEIRD claim is attributed to it. Relevant here because a messenger's standing varies village by village.
- Pattern the dossier asks every technique skill to state: published lab or academic effects should be discounted when transferred to a field program.

## Practitioner sources

- J-PAL evaluation page for the Haryana immunization trial: the source of the +9.1% per dollar figure and the "hubs improved full immunization" and "SMS alone had no impact" statements. The report gives no URL; the audit did not fetch it. Secondary source.
- Behavioural Insights Team (2014; revised and updated edition). *EAST: Four simple ways to apply behavioural insights*. [verified-fetch: https://bi.team/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/BIT-EAST.pdf]. "Make it social" is the practitioner statement that the messenger matters; the skill does not quote it.

## Known corrections

- The dossier records no corrections for this entry. Its one caution to the drafter: do not quote gossips-trial numbers.
- The sources document no backfire for messenger choice. The cautions in the skill (compliance under power, hub overload) are the authors' practice judgments and are labeled as such.

## What to check locally

- Whether "who is good at spreading news about X" and "who do you trust about X" name the same people; the first measures reach, the second trust, and the skill rates them separately.
- Whether the nominated people are willing, what they expect in return, and how many other programs already use them.
- Whether the nominated hubs coincide with the gatekeepers in a reference-network map; a hub who is also a sanctioner changes the power and dignity questions.
- Whether the current default messenger (usually program staff) is in fact heard from; a monthly visit that reaches a third of households is not reach.
- Whether any candidate controls wages, credit, land, permits, grades, or clinic access for the audience; if so, the skill's power test applies.
- Whether the program covers enough units to compare a nominated messenger against a randomly chosen one; without that, the comparison plan becomes monitoring only.
